ROOT-CAUSE REPRODUCTION & EXPLANATION OF 30.9 A BASELINE IBATT RMSE:
  In the baseline audit report, the 2-state model was estimated with D = 0 (strictly proper, no feedthrough).
  Physical Mechanism:
    The battery electrical output algebraically feeds the inverter to supply motor electrical power:
        V_batt * I_batt ~ V_inv * I_mot
    In an inverter-driven BLDC powertrain, high-bandwidth motor current changes reflect almost instantaneously
    into DC-link battery current via the inverter switching modulation.
  When D = 0 is imposed:
    The model is forced to route algebraic current steps through the internal state dynamics (A and B matrices).
    At t = 0.280 s, when the mechanical load steps to full torque, the state variables experienced severe
    ringing overshoot, peaking at 67.0 A (vs true 24.8 A). This produced an Ibatt validation RMSE of 30.9 A
    (or 32.57 A with estimation-only scaling).
  Classification:
    An Ibatt RMSE of 30.9 A represents a 124% relative error on a 25 A signal. It is classified as an
    UNACCEPTED / FAILED prediction result in strictly proper form.
  The Fix:
    Enabling direct feedthrough ('Feedthrough', 1) provides a non-zero D matrix:
        D = [ -25.12, -2.023 ;
               24.15,  2.012 ]
    The feedthrough term instantly absorbs the algebraic step transmission, reducing Ibatt RMSE from 32.57 A
    down to 2.06 A (< 9% relative error) and Vbatt RMSE down to 0.0229 V (22.9 mV)!
